## The problem

Standard RAG systems rely on flat semantic search, forget earlier turns in the conversation, and hallucinate citations.

## What I built

A custom architecture instead of LangChain's ConversationalRetrievalChain: a lightweight query rewriter in front of a hybrid pipeline.

- BM25 and dense retrieval fused with Reciprocal Rank Fusion
- A cross-encoder reranker
- A confidence gate that refuses to answer when the top similarity score is below 0.35
- Citations assembled in code, never generated by the model

## The hard part

I designed frozen `PipelineResult`, `Citation` and `Timings` dataclasses as the single contract between the pipeline and every consumer: the UI, the FastAPI service and the RAGAS evaluation. Because of that, adding the FastAPI layer in week three needed no refactoring.

## Outcome

- A 278-question gold dataset, generated with an LLM-assisted pipeline and verified by hand.
- A 75-question stratified benchmark scored with RAGAS on faithfulness, answer relevancy and context recall.
- Custom metrics for citation accuracy, query-rewrite accuracy and confidence-gate accuracy.
