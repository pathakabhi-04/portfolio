## The problem

Surveillance video is unsearchable. Investigators scrub through hours of footage by hand, and nothing connects computer vision detection to natural-language questions.

## What I built

A full pipeline from video ingestion to cited answers.

**Vision side.** Frame extraction with FFmpeg and OpenCV, a fine-tuned YOLOv8m detector, ByteTrack tracking, and a rule-based Event Candidate Engine covering six event types, including near-miss, collision, sudden stop, restricted-zone entry and pedestrian interaction. Keyframes are selected and captioned with LLaVA-7B in 4-bit.

**Retrieval side.** Hybrid retrieval with BM25 and ChromaDB fused by Reciprocal Rank Fusion, a temporal reranker, and grounded answer generation through Groq with timestamp and frame citations.

## The hard part

**Candidate and confirmed incidents.** The rule engine proposes candidates with confidence scores, and LLaVA confirms or downgrades them. This keeps false positives from reaching the retrieval layer.

**Indian roads.** I fine-tuned YOLOv8m on IDD-117K so it detects auto-rickshaws, two-wheelers and cattle that COCO-trained models miss.
